What the rules engine catches

Before anything reaches Odoo, every line is checked against deterministic rules with a suggested fix — so the exception queue is actionable, not a pile of "AI is unsure".

Pack sizes & multiples

Qty 5 on a case-of-4 product gets flagged with the next valid multiple.

Min / max order quantities

Per-product bounds from your catalog.

Price deviation

Unit price vs. catalog price beyond your tolerance — flagged, never silently accepted.

Duplicate POs

Same customer + PO number already processed? It lands in the exception queue.

Weak matches

Every customer and SKU match carries a score. Low scores get a human, not a guess.

Currency & completeness

Allowed currencies, missing quantities, low-confidence extraction — all surfaced.
