# The Partner Program

The single most important commercial decision in the plan: **we do not cold-
call manufacturers one by one.** Odoo implementation partners already own the
relationships with dozens or hundreds of manufacturing/wholesale/distribution
clients. The partner becomes the acquisition channel.

## The pitch to a partner

Not: *"Would you like to buy my AI?"*

Instead:

> **"Do any of your manufacturing clients still manually enter emailed
> customer purchase orders into Odoo?"**

They almost certainly have at least three. Then:

1. We run the two-minute demo on their laptop (or a hosted demo link).
2. They show it to the client whose order-entry staff is drowning.
3. The client pays us; the partner earns **20–30% recurring** for the life of
   the subscription.
4. The partner gets a product that makes them look forward-thinking — they
   keep the client stickier, and they have a recurring revenue stream of
   their own.

## Why partners say yes

- **Recurring, not one-shot.** $88–119/month per Business-plan client,
  every month. 20 clients ≈ $1,800–2,400/month of passive revenue.
- **Zero delivery burden.** We install, we run onboarding, we handle
  support. The partner's only job is the introduction and the first
  conversation.
- **Differentiator in renewals.** "Your clients get AI order entry" is a
  line in their renewal deck.
- **We do the demo, they do the handshake.** We provide the demo
  environment, the one-pager, and the client-facing language.

## Partner terms

| Item | Term |
|---|---|
| Commission | 20% standard / 25% for partners referring 5+ clients / 30% founding partners (first 12 months) |
| Recurrence | For the life of the subscription, prorated monthly |
| Onboarding fee | Partner earns 20% of the one-time onboarding fee too |
| Upsell | Commission recalculates on plan upgrades automatically |
| Churn | Commission stops the month the client churns (prorated) |
| Attribution | Partner codes clients at onboarding; disputes resolved by first-registered contact |
| Co-branding | Partners may white-label the console header for their clients |

## Partner enablement kit (what we send them)

1. **Two-minute demo** — `docker compose up`, done (or a hosted demo URL)
2. **One-pager** (this repo's landing page, printable)
3. **Outreach email** — a 6-line email they can paste to their clients
   (see SALES.md)
4. **ROI calculator** — the anchor math from PRICING.md, pre-filled
5. **Objection handling** — "what about our scanned POs?", "will the AI
   make mistakes?", "does our data leave our network?"
6. **Quarterly updates** — what shipped, what clients are saying

## Finding partners

- Odoo partner directories (odoo.com/partners), filtered to
  manufacturing/wholesale/distribution verticals in Canada and the US
- ERP practice leads on LinkedIn (search: "Odoo" + "manufacturing")
- Upwork/Fiverr profiles that list Odoo + order entry / OCR projects
- The buyer requests themselves — the demand scan already found buyers
  paying $50–6,000 for exactly this workflow; many are partners shopping
  for a tool to resell

## Partner funnel

```
partner directory (≈1,500 NA partners)
        │  vertical filter (mfg/wholesale/distribution)
        ▼
  ~300 plausible partners
        │  outreach: 6-line email, 2 touches
        ▼
  ~45 open conversations      (15%)
        │  two-minute demo
        ▼
  ~18 interested partners     (40%)
        │  we equip them, they introduce clients
        ▼
  ~9 active partners          (50%)
        │  3 client intros each on average
        ▼
  ~27 client implementations
  ≈ $8–10k MRR at year one    (before churn)
```

Conservative on every step; the plan's target is **3 paid implementations**
to prove the model, and this channel exists to make 3 easy and 30 likely.
